## Solution comment (post at 5pm ET, then pin)

The flaw: line 3.

Unlevered free cash flow belongs to ALL capital providers — debt and equity — so it gets discounted at the blended cost of that capital: WACC. The cost of equity prices cash flows that belong to shareholders alone (a levered DCF), and that path lands on equity value directly — no bridge required.

Discount UFCF at the cost of equity and you've mismatched rate and cash flow: for any company carrying debt, the cost of equity sits above WACC, so the valuation comes out too low.

Corrected line 3: "I discount the cash flows and the terminal value back at WACC."
