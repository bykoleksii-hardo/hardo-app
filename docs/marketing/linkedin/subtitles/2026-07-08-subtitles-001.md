Subtitles #001 — interviewer-speak edition

What they said:
"Are you sure?"

What you heard:
"That was wrong, and now we both know it."

What it means:
More often than not: "Your answer was fine. I want to see if it survives contact."

Bankers defend numbers under pushback for a living. An interviewer who pokes a correct answer usually isn't correcting you — they're checking whether you fold.

Hold-or-fold protocol:
→ Take the beat. Re-run the logic before you speak — a short pause reads as diligence, not doubt.
→ If it holds: "Yes — and here's why," then re-anchor the one step that matters.
→ If you catch a real slip: correct it once, cleanly. No apology spiral — self-correction scores.

Candidates who hear a verdict talk themselves out of right answers.
Candidates who hear a test check the work and hold.
