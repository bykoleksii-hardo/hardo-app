THE TAPE #003 — fictional candidate, real mistake.

Sunday night. Mock interview with a mentor.

Mentor: "Why investment banking?"
Candidate: "Honestly? My LinkedIn feed convinced me everyone else already has an offer. Fear is a powerful motivator."
[notes: honest. not an answer.]
Mentor: "That's a reason to recruit. It's not a reason for banking. Try again."
Candidate: "...steep learning curve?"
[notes: now the feed is writing his answers.]
Mentor: "Whose answer is that?"

Where it turned: he stopped answering for the feed and started answering for himself.

What the tape should have said:
Fear tells you to recruit. It can't tell you why THIS job.
A real answer names a trigger (a class, a deal you followed, a project), shows you acted on it, and ties it to the actual day-to-day — live transactions, modeling reps, client exposure.
And the feed? Survivorship bias with a UI. Offers get posted. Rejections don't.
