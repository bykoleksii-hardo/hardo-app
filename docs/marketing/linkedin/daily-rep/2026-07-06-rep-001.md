"Walk me through a DCF" is not a valuation question.
It's a structure-under-pressure question.

Daily Rep #001 — Technical Rep

The question:
"Walk me through a DCF."

Why they ask it:
Not to check you memorized a formula — to see whether you can organize a
complex process into a clean, confident 90 seconds.

The strong answer (structure, not a script):
→ Project unlevered free cash flows for 5–10 years (EBIT → NOPAT, + D&A,
− CapEx, − change in NWC).
→ Estimate terminal value — Gordon growth or exit multiple. Most of the value
usually sits here; say so.
→ Discount the cash flows and terminal value at WACC to get enterprise value.
→ Bridge to equity value (subtract net debt) and divide by shares outstanding.

Where candidates lose points:
Diving into WACC subscripts before giving the big picture. Headline first,
detail on request.

Your rep: answer it out loud. 60 seconds. No notes.
